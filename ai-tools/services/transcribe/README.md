### Feature highlights

* **Accuracy and speed** competitive with commercial offerings
* Supports **16 languages**
* Differentiates different speakers (**speaker diarization**)
* **Edit transcriptions**
* Supports transcription in **plain text** `.txt`) or Microsoft Word (`.docx`) formats
* Supports captions/subtitles in **SubRip** (`.srt`) and **WebVTT** (`.vtt`) formats
* **Translation** between 15 languages

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Any** machine-produced transcriptions, including those from _Transcribe_, must be reviewed and revised by a human to meet digital accessibility requirements. See [getting-better-transcriptions.md](getting-better-transcriptions.md "mention") and [comparing-speech-to-text-models.md](comparing-speech-to-text-models.md "mention") for best practices and model details.
{% endhint %}

## Data Privacy and Retention Policy

Brown must store your data on your behalf to do transcription services for you and play back your file while you review the work. For more information on how your data is processed and stored, please see [Transcribe Data Handling](../../data-privacy/transcribe-data-handling-level-3/).

{% hint style="warning" %}
_Transcribe_ only stores audio and transcription data **temporarily**. It is not a long-term storage service. Download and securely store your transcriptions as soon as they are available. See [downloading-transcriptions](downloading-transcriptions/ "mention") for more information.\
OIT/CCV automatically deletes _Transcribe_ audio after **7 days** and reserves the right to clear _Transcribe_ of all data as needed. OIT/CCV is not responsible for any data loss resulting from use of this service. \
The [Brown University Code of Conduct](https://policy.brown.edu/policy/code-conduct) applies to _Transcribe_ and all IT services at Brown.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

## Usage Limit

Each user has a daily usage limit of **20 hours** of total audio/video files. Once this limit is reached, the user will have to wait until the next day to submit more transcription jobs.
