# Upcoming services

At CCV, we continue to update the AI services that we provide to offer the best functionalities and user experiences. Here's a sneak peak of what's coming to CCV AI services:

* **LibreChat:** We will be adding new AI models to LibreChat, including the latest models with reasoning capabilities, such as \`claude-3.7-sonnet-reasoning\`, and \`deepseek-r1\`.
* Ask Oscar: We will enrich the documentation to which Ask Oscar has access to enrich the knowledge of the AI assistant.

We are listening to our users! If you have any feedback on what we should be working on in the future, please feel free to email us at [help@brown.edu](mailto:help@brown.edu) or [support@ccv.brown.edu](mailto:support@ccv.brown.edu)
