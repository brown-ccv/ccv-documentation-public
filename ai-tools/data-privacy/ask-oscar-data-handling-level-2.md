# Ask Oscar Data Handling (Level 2)

## Ask Oscar

As of February 2025, we do not collect or store any user data for this tool, and we have no plan to change that. Any chat messages that the users provide and the output generated by AI models are not stored by the service. All AI models that we use for this service comes from Vertex AI on Google Cloud Platform and strictly follows the [Zero Retention Policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/vertex-ai-zero-data-retention), which guarantees that your prompts (inputs) and completions (outputs), your embeddings, and your training data:

* are NOT available to other customers.
* are NOT available to Google.
* are NOT used to improve Google models and all managed models on Google Cloud.
