# CCV Oscar Bootcamp

Join us from **3-5pm on 4-6 October 2021** for a series of interactive online tutorials designed to introduce new users to [Oscar](https://ccv.brown.edu/services/oscar/), Brown's research computing cluster. Members of [CCV](https://ccv.brown.edu)'s High-Performance Computing (HPC) team will provide participants with the practical knowledge necessary to make effective use of Oscar in their own research, while introducing the fundamentals of high-performance computing.

A complete list of tutorials, along with brief descriptions, can be found on the schedule for each day in the menu to the left.

## Registration

Bootcamp tutorials are offered on an _à la carte_ basis, so that participants need only attend those tutorials that are of interest to them. Registered participants will receive an email with instructions for connecting to the tutorials via Zoom on the morning of each session.

[Click here to register for the bootcamp](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScrsgCd2emMncpeqFUw2576YIYI_jXkU_5nycV-8WxxyMQnDA/viewform?usp=sf_link)

## Oscar accounts

We highly recommend that participants have an account on Oscar, since many of the exercises in the tutorials will require access to Oscar. Accounts on Oscar are free to all members of the Brown community. If you do not already have an account on Oscar, you can apply for one by following the link below.
