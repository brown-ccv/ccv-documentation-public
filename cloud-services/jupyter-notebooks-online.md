# Jupyter Notebooks Online

JupyterNotebooks have become a popular interface for teaching scientific programming. While there are many products and vendors available that host pre-configured notebook environments. At Brown, we directly can support you with the following ones:

## Google Colab

Colaboratory, or "Colab" for short, allows you to write and execute Python in your browser, with 

* Zero configuration required
* Free access to GPUs
* Easy sharing

Whether you're a **student**, a **data scientist** or an **AI researcher**, Colab can make your work easier. Watch [Introduction to Colab](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inN8seMm7UI) to learn more or visit [https://colab.research.google.com](https://colab.research.google.com)
