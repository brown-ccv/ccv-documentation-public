## FAQ

## Can I use my personal email ID to create a Globus account?

Yes. You can create a [Globus ID](https://www.globusid.org/create) and link your personal email in the profile. However, Brown resources are not accessible using Globus ID directly.

## How can research collaborators at other organizations access my data?

A researcher at Brown can create a Globus collection for their data and share it with research collaborators at other organizations. Collaborators need to have a Globus account created either using their organization ID or Globus ID.
