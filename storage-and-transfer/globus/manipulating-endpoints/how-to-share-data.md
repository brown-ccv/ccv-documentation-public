# Share Data

## Sharing with a user or a Group:

{% hint style="info" %}
If you do not already have a group or you want to create a new group with whom you will share data, follow the [official Globus documentation](https://docs.globus.org/how-to/managing-groups/) for group creation.
{% endhint %}

To share data/collections with a group, follow the [official Globus documentation](https://docs.globus.org/how-to/share-files/).

{% hint style="warning" %}
in Step 5, the two 'Add Permissions - Share With' figures should have one more option: public (anonymous), as shown in the figure below:
{% endhint %}

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (47).png>)

## Sharing Using Links

To share data/collections using a link, click on the link icon:

![The 'Get Link' button (highlighted in yellow).](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png>)

A new menu should appear in the center of the screen:

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png>)

Select from the download/access options and then click on the blue clipboard icon to the right of the link to copy the link.

{% hint style="success" %}
You can now share the file/folder you selected by sharing that link with other Globus users.
{% endhint %}
