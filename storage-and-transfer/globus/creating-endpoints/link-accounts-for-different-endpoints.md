# Link Accounts for Different Endpoints

If you have two endpoints which are created using different accounts, you need to link the two accounts before transferring data between the two endpoints. Here we demonstrate that to link an endpoint from Globus Connect Personal and an endpoint from Oscar.

1\. Log into your Globus Connect Persona, click the 'Account' icon in the left panel, and then click the 'Link Another Identity' button (Figure 1).

![Figure 1. Account](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2020-11-01 at 9.16.09 AM.png>)

2\. Select 'Brown University' and then click the 'Continue' button (Figure 2).

![Figure 2. Select Identity](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2020-11-01 at 9.16.26 AM.png>)

3\. Your two accounts are confirmed to be linked (Figure 3).

![Figure 3. Confirmation](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2020-11-01 at 9.16.59 AM.png>)
