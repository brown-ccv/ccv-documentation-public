# Login Procedure

Once the storage team confirms that the requested 'zone' is in place, please follow these steps to log in to the Starfish tool and access the zone.

**Step 1:** Connect to Brown VPN and go to [https://starfish.brown.edu/](https://starfish.brown.edu/). We recommend using Google Chrome.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Brown VPN is required to access Hibernate Service.
{% endhint %}

**Step 2:** Make sure <mark style="color:green;">`Network authentication (PAM)`</mark> is selected and sign in using your Brown AD credentials

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-01-12 at 11.30.48 AM.png" alt="Screenshot of Starfish Login page"><figcaption><p>Starfish Sign-in</p></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3:** Upon login, you should see your zones (top left) and associated directories on the landing page. Navigate into the sub-directories to view your files.
